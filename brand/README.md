# The Facedown mark

A spade whose heart is a keyhole.

- **The spade** is the game.
- **The keyhole** is the secret. Your two cards are sealed to a key that only your browser holds; nobody else, the dealer's operator included, can open them.
- **The slot runs into the spade's own stem.** The lock isn't stamped on top of the card, it is the card's shape. That is the whole design of Facedown: privacy built into the deal, not added after.

The same mark is printed on the back of every face-down card at the table.

Colours: card red `#a3262c`, card white `#fbfaf7`.
